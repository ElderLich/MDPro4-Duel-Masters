# Attribution and provenance

MDPro4-Duel Masters is a community continuation of
[YGOPro-DM](https://github.com/szefo09/ygopro-dm), originally developed by
**Hello23-Ygopro** and **szefo09** (also recorded as Szefo09).
The local source was commit `a047df999a2109f52a065fc9760459a0780560b2`,
dated 12 July 2019. Its original GPL version 3 license is retained in `LICENSE`.
The upstream repository and its Git history remain the attribution authority.

The first commit in this repository preserves 1,067 original working-tree files
byte for byte. `textures` and `DataEditorX` were excluded at the maintainer's
request. The tag `upstream/a047df9` identifies this imported baseline. The original
README remains available in that baseline commit.
Later commits identify the MDPro4 loader, rule, usability and tooling changes.

The original project builds on [YGOPro](https://github.com/Fluorohydride/ygopro)
and its card scripting/core ecosystem. MDPro4 supplies the current host, renderer
and native duel core; those components are not copied into this repository.

Duel Masters names, card text and artwork belong to their respective rights
holders. The upstream notices credit Shogakukan and Mitsui & Co. for the Official
Card Game, and Wizards of the Coast for the Trading Card Game. This project is
unofficial. Its source-code license does not assert ownership of those assets.
Supplied card scans remain external build inputs; mapping receipts retain their
relative source paths and SHA-256 values. No texture or DataEditorX assets are
included in the fork or expansion.
